# Privacy Policy for Respawn

_Last updated: November 7, 2026_

Respawn ("the app") is a web browser for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## Information we collect
**None.** The developer of Respawn does not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting service and no advertising.

## Information stored on your device
To work as a browser, Respawn keeps the following **only on your device**:
- Browsing history (used for address-bar suggestions). You can turn it off or clear it in the app menu.
- Open tabs, tab names and saved account names, so they can be restored.
- Cookies, logins and site data saved by the websites you visit, kept separately for each tab.
- App settings.

This information never leaves your device except as part of your normal use of websites (for example, a website receives the cookies it set). Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

## Websites you visit
When you visit a website, that website (and services it uses) may collect information under its own privacy policy. Respawn's built-in blocker stops many known advertising and tracking services, but it can't block everything. Please review the privacy policies of the websites you use.

## Downloads
Files you choose to download are saved to your device's Downloads folder using Android's download manager.

## Children
Respawn is not directed at children under 13.
