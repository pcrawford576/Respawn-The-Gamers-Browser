## Short description (max 80 characters)

## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Respawn, The Gamers Browser, is a lightweight web browser made for playing browser games on your phone or tablet.

MULTIPLE ACCOUNTS, ONE APP
• Every tab can keep its own separate login, so you can stay signed in to several game accounts at once.
• Name your accounts and switch between them from the account list at the top.
• Named accounts are remembered, so you can load them again even after closing their tab.

BUILT FOR PLAYING
• Swipe the account bar left or right to switch between loaded tabs.
• Background tabs pause but stay loaded, so your game is still there when you come back.
• If a browser game crashes, Respawn reloads it automatically.
• Fullscreen mode and keep-screen-on for long sessions.
• Desktop-site mode for games made for computer browsers.

FAST AND CLEAN
• Built-in ad and tracker blocker.
• Address-bar suggestions from sites you visit.
• Your tabs come back when you reopen the app.
• Gaming sidebar with chat, streams, videos and game stores one swipe away.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Respawn has no account, no analytics and no ads of its own. Your history, tabs and logins stay on your device.

Respawn is an independent browser and is not affiliated with any game publisher.

## Answers for "App content" forms
- **Privacy policy**: required. Host `privacy-policy.md` online (see that file) and paste the link.
- **Ads**: No, the app contains no ads.
- **Data safety**: The app does **not collect or share** any user data with the developer. Everything (history, tabs, logins) is stored only on the device. Answer "No" to data collection and sharing.
- **Content rating**: complete the questionnaire. Answer **yes** to "unrestricted internet access" (it's a web browser).
- **Target audience**: 13+ or 18+ (don't target children; a web browser gives open internet access).
- **App access**: all features available without special access.

## Avoid
Don't put game names or logos (e.g. "Total Battle") in the app name, icon, screenshots' captions or
description. Google can reject listings that use other companies' trademarks.
