Discord & translator

In a Discord tab: translations appear under foreign-language messages. Alt+click a message to translate it, Alt+T translates what you're typing into the "Reply in" language. The free DeepL plan includes 500,000 characters a month.

Logins

Every tab remembers the account it's signed in to and always reopens to it, so several tabs can play different accounts of the same game. Links opened from a tab use that tab's login. Closing a tab forgets its login.

Keyboard shortcuts

Click a shortcut, then press the keys you want. Esc cancels, Backspace removes it. Shortcuts work everywhere in Respawn, including inside games. Single keys (like Q) still type normally in text boxes.

History

Delete everything Respawn remembers

Resource limiter

Background tabs always hibernate: they stay loaded (games keep running) but use minimal CPU and RAM.

Ad & tracker blocker

Items marked restart apply next time Respawn starts.

Rename tab

Saving a name remembers this account, so you can reopen it from Accounts ▾ even after closing the tab.
